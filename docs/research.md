# Research: real theme-park complaints

Gathered from search on 2026-10-18 for comedy material. Paraphrased; links at the bottom.
Google, TripAdvisor and Yelp review pages couldn't be fetched directly, so treat single
anecdotes as anecdotes.

## Worlds of Fun context

- Sold by Six Flags in spring 2026 to EPR Properties; Enchanted Parks runs it now. Same
  name, confused branding, refunds handled badly in the handover ("that's the old
  company's policy").
- One 2026 first-timer counted half the coasters down for part of the day; one coaster
  "repeatedly failed to open" until about 7:30 PM. Most coasters ran a single train, with
  gates held shut until the whole station emptied.
- 2024 maintenance union said crews were short-staffed, parts weren't ordered, and
  morning inspections were thin. Earlier years the park closed weekdays for lack of staff.
- 2025: Haunt mazes that used to be included became a $15–25 upcharge, called "an exciting
  new evolution."
- A family owed a $700 dining-pass refund was thanked for their patience repeatedly
  for months. Make-goods for a three-ride day included a souvenir cup.
- A coaster fenced off for multi-year rehab, switching contractors mid-way.
- Haunt monsters seen on break, out of character.

## General 1-star patterns

Ranked roughly by frequency: rides down/closed; one-train ops and long lines; staffing;
upcharges and pass bait-and-switch; closed food stands and bad/expensive food; refunds and
customer service; mid-ride stops; security/crowd chaos; prices (water, parking, lockers);
paid line-skip resentment; heat; apps showing rides open that are closed; lockers billed
by the minute when the line runs long; bags coming out the other side of the locker bank.
